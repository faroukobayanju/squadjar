## Problem Statement

Nigerian students run rotating savings (ajo/esusu) and class or departmental dues through one person's bank account plus a WhatsApp list. The treasurer or an early recipient disappears, ticks get forged, and everyone after them loses. Existing apps (Corral, Ajo, Kashlet) digitize the ledger but still let an admin hold the cash, mark unpaid members as paid, or swap payout accounts (Simpa Labs test, Aug 2026).

Target user: Nigerian undergraduates (18 to 24) in class, department, or friend circles of 5 to 20 people, organized by a class rep.

## What Makes This Cool

- Nobody holds the money. The circle contract holds the pot and pays out on schedule by itself.
- A defaulter's deposit covers their missed payment, so the recipient still gets the full pot (except in the rare case where a Reliable member's capped deposit runs out).
- Payout order is earned. A portable trust score (an "ajo credit history") follows you across circles, and reliable people get early slots and smaller deposits.
- Kimi writes payment nudges in English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo, or Hausa, and drafts the WhatsApp message a rep sends to shame late payers.
- Zero crypto surface: email or Google login, naira amounts, no wallet, no gas, no addresses.

## Premises (agreed)

1. The real pain is the treasurer holding the money, not saving itself. Pitch: "no one holds the money."
2. Class reps are the wedge. One rep brings 10 to 20 people.
3. Collateral plus automatic payout beats reminders. That is the gap with existing apps.
4. Testnet with simulated top-up is acceptable if the contract logic is real and the fiat gap is named honestly.
5. A cross-circle trust score is the long-term moat, not just a demo feature.

## Approaches Considered

- A, Minimal (core circles only, no Kimi): rejected, loses the Kimi bounty and the nudges that drive retention.
- C, Link-first (no dashboard, every action a WhatsApp link): rejected as the main shape, too risky for the judge walkthrough. Its one-tap pay link idea is kept.

## Recommended Approach: B, full plan plus one-tap pay links

### Contracts (Solidity, Foundry, Monad testnet)

- `AjoNGN`: ERC-20 test naira with `faucet()` capped per call.
- `CircleFactory`: deploys circles. Stores the trust record per user (`paidOnTime`, `paidLate`, `missed`, `circlesCompleted`). Only factory-created circles can write to it. `trustScore = onTime - 2*late - 10*missed + 3*completed`. Tiers: New (<5), Building (5 to 19), Reliable (20+).
- `Circle` lifecycle:
  1. Open: members `join()` (max 20). Members can leave and the organizer can cancel before start.
  2. Start: organizer starts with at least 3 members. Order is by trust score, ties broken by `keccak256(prevrandao, circle, member)` (verify Monad `prevrandao` behaviour on day 1). Each member locks a deposit within the deposit window. Anyone who misses the window is dropped, and order and deposits are recomputed once (the window restarts only for those whose deposit changed).
  3. Deposit for payout position p (1-based) of n, contribution c: `c*(n-p)`, which is exactly what they still owe after being paid. The last member deposits 0. For Reliable members it is capped at `3c`.
  4. Rounds: members `contribute()`, with one `approve(max)` to the circle at join. `settleRound()` runs when all have paid, or after deadline + grace (callable by anyone). The payout is pushed by ERC-20 transfer inside `settleRound` (safe: `AjoNGN` has no hooks).
     - A payment inside the grace window counts as `paidLate`.
     - A miss **before** your own payout is netted from your own upcoming payout.
     - A miss **after** your payout comes out of your deposit.
     - Refill rule: if `deposit < c * roundsRemaining`, the member must top it up before the next round, or is treated as defaulting on all remaining rounds.
  5. Uncovered losses (only possible above the Reliable 3c cap) reduce later pots evenly. The UI names the defaulter.
  6. End: honest members get their deposit back. The defaulter's leftover deposit is split among members who paid every round. Rounding dust goes to the last recipient.
  7. No organizer powers after start.
- Trust score is `int256` (can go negative). Only circles with 5+ members and `c >= ₦1,000` count toward `circlesCompleted`. Fake-account farming is a known testnet limitation and is named in the pitch.
- Timing per period:

  | Period | Round length | Grace | Deposit window |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Demo (hidden) | 5 min | 1 min | 5 min |
  | Weekly | 7 days | 12 h | 48 h |
  | Monthly | 30 days | 48 h | 72 h |

- Fuzz invariant: `sum(contributions + deposits) == sum(payouts + refunds + forfeits paid out) + contract balance`, after every action.

### App (Next.js PWA on Vercel)

- Privy: email + Google login, embedded wallet, native gas sponsorship on Monad testnet, a server wallet as the settle relayer.
- Language: "circle", "contribution", "payout", "deposit (refundable)". Never "wallet", "stake", "gas", or "transaction".
- Screens: Login, Home (₦ balance, my circles, trust badge), Add money (card-style checkout, labelled test mode, calls the faucet), Create circle (form plus Kimi text box), Invite (WhatsApp share), Join, Circle detail (round timeline, public paid ticks, Pay button, deposit status), Payout celebration, Profile (trust explainer, receipts link to the explorer in an advanced section).
- One-tap pay page: every reminder deep-links to `/c/<slug>/pay`, which opens straight to the Pay button.
- One approval when joining. Each payment is then one sponsored transaction.

### Backend

- Postgres (Neon via the Vercel Marketplace): `users` (Privy ID, name, email, language, address), `circles` (address, display name, invite slug), `notifications` (log for dedup).
- Chain is the source of truth for money, membership, order and trust. Reads via viem `multicall`, no indexer.
- cron-job.org calls `/api/cron/settle` and `/api/cron/nudge` every minute (it works around the Vercel Hobby daily cron limit). The circle page also settles on view if the deadline has passed. All settles are signed by the Privy server wallet relayer. Keep it topped up with testnet MON and alert when it runs low.
- Nudge schedule: T-24h, T-1h, and after a miss (demo period: T-2m, T-30s). Deduplicated by `(member, round, stage)`.

### Kimi (OpenAI-compatible API, tool calling)

1. Creation agent: turns plain text in any supported language into a circle draft using `draftCircle` and `lookupTrust`. It asks follow-up questions when something is missing and warns about order conflicts. The user confirms on the normal form. Kimi never moves money.
2. Nudge agent: reads circle state through `getCircleState` and `getMemberHistory`, then writes a personal reminder in each member's language. Delivered in-app and by email (Resend). Web push is deferred to days 9-10. It escalates near the deadline.
3. "Remind the group" button: Kimi drafts a group message, and the organizer shares it through a `wa.me` link.
4. Stretch: a circle assistant chat that answers only from tool reads.

Fallbacks: if Kimi fails, the plain form still works and nudges use an English template.
